Instructions about Clearance from 
NTC Department 
 
 
Students must have to register on Alumni portal before coming to NTC Department for 
clearance. The procedure is as follows: 
1. Open the link alumni.must.edu.pk 
2. Click on register/login 
3. Fill the form by providing necessary details 
4. Password will be sent to the provided Email address 
5. After successful registration and verification, Clearance forms will be signed
