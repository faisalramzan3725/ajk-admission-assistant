Eligibility Criteria for Admission in Undergraduate Programs Fall-2026 
Faculty of Engineering & Technology Programs:  
(Civil, Computer Systems, Electrical, Mechanical and Software Engineering) 
 
Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc.(Pre-medical)/A-levels/ICS or equivalent/ DAE in 
relevant field for all Programs with at least 60% marks and 33% marks in entry test conducted by PEC 
approved testing bodies.  
Mirpur Institute of Technology (MIT) Programs:  
(Architectural Engineering Technology, Engineering Technology (Artificial Intelligence),  
Biomedical Engineering Technology, Civil  Engineering Technology, Electrical  Engineering 
Technology and Information Engineering Technology) 
 
For Architectural  Engineering Technology : Must have passed F .Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc. (Pre -
Medical)/ICS/DAE (Civil/ Architecture) or equivalent with at least 50% marks.  
For Engineering Technology (Artificial Intelligence ): Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc. 
(Pre-Medical)/ICS or equivalent/DAE in relevant field with at least 50% marks.  
For B.Sc. Biomedical Engineering Technology : Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc. (Pre-
Medical)/ICS or equivalent/DAE in relevant field with at least 50% marks. 
For Civil & Electrical Engineering Tech nology: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc. (Pre-
Medical)/ICS or equivalent/DAE in relevant field with at least 50% marks.  
For B.Sc. Information Engineering Technology : Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Engineering)/F.Sc. (Pre-
Medical)/ICS or equivalent/DAE in relevant field with at least 50% marks.  
(Note:  For admission in above mentioned programs students must appear in state level 
entry test conducted by MUST as approved testing body from PEC)  
Faculty of Natural & Applied Sciences Programs:  
Biotechnology, Botany, Chemistry, Computer Science, Environmental Science, Forestry, Information 
Technology, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics and Zoology 
 
For Biotechnology: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre -Medical) with at least 50% marks. No D -Grade in 
academic record.  
For Botany: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre-Medical) or equivalent with at least 45% marks. No D-Grade 
in academic record. 
For Chemistry: Must have passed F.Sc. (With Chemistry) or equivalent with at least 45% marks. No D-
Grade in academic record.  
For CS and IT: Must have passed Intermediate/12 years schooling/A- Level (HSSC) or Equivalent with 
mathematics having at least 50% marks. The students who have not studied  Mathematics at 
intermediate level have to pass two deficiency courses of Mathematics (06 credits) in first two 
semesters. 
For Environmental Science: Intermediate Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS (only with physics and 
chemistry) or IBCC equivalent degree  (A-Level, DAE) in any science group with any of subjects i.e., 
Biology, General Biology, Mathematics, Chemistry or Physics with minimum 45% marks. No 3rd Division 
in Intermediate. 
For Forestry: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre-Medical/ Pre-Engineering/ ICS) with at least 45% marks.  
For Mathematics: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre-Engineering)/ ICS or Intermediate or equivalent degree 
with science at least 45% marks. No D-Grade in academic record.  

For Physics: Must have passed Intermediate or equivalent with physics having at least 45% marks from 
an accredited institution. OR Three years engineering diploma with math and physics. No D -grade in 
academic career. 
For Statistics: Intermediate with minimum 45% marks (F.Sc. Pre -Engineering or I.Com or General 
Science Group with Statistics). 
For Zoology: Must have passed F.Sc. (Pre-Medical) with at least 45% marks. No D -Grade in academic 
record. 
Eligibility Criteria for Admission in BS 5th Semester Programs Admission Fall-2026  
Faculty of Natural & Applied Sciences BS 5th Semester Programs:  
Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics, and Zoology:  
 
For Chemistry, Physics and Zoology: Must have studied the relevant subject with 200 marks in the last 
degree (B.Sc./ ADS or equivalent) and overall with at least 2nd division.  
For Mathematics: Must have studied Mathematics with 400 marks in the last degree (B.Sc./ ADS or 
equivalent) and overall with at least 2nd division.  
Faculty of Health & Medical Sciences Programs:  
Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm D), Doctor of Physical  Therapy, Human Nutrition and Dietetics, 
Medical Lab Technology and Microbiology: 
 
For Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D): Must have passed F.Sc.(Pre-Medical or equivalent) with at least 
60% marks. 
For Doctor of Physical Therapy: Must have passed F.Sc.(Pre-Medical or equivalent) with at least 60% 
marks. 
For BS Human Nutrition and Dietetics: Must have passed F.Sc.(Pre-Medical or equivalent) with at least 
60% marks. 
For BS Medical Lab Technology: Must have passed F.Sc.(Pre-Medical or equivalent) with at least 60% 
marks. 
For BS Microbiology: Must have passed F.Sc.(Pre-Medical or equivalent) with at least 50% marks. 
Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities Programs: 
Arabic, B.A. LLB, Education, English, Fashion and Design, Home Economics, International Relations, 
Islamic Studies, Media and Communication Studies,  Political Science,  Sociology and Tourism & 
Hospitality:  
 
Must have passed F.A/ F.Sc./ I.Com/ ICS or equivalent with at least 45% marks.  
For B.A. LLB: Must have passed F.A/ F.Sc. or equivalent (as per university policy for BS or equivalent 
education) with at least 45% marks and have passed LAT as per the HEC requirements.   
Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities BS 5th Semester Programs:  
Education, English and Islamic Studies: 
 
Must have passed BA/ B.Sc./ BS Commerce/ ADE/ ADS/ ADA or equivalent with at least 2nd division or 
with CGPA 2.5/4.0. 
Faculty of Management Sciences Programs:  
Accounting & Finance, Banking and Finance (BBF), Business Administration (BBA), BS Commerce: 
 
Must have passed F.A/ F.Sc./ I.Com/ ICS or equivalent with at least 45% marks.  
Faculty of Management Sciences BS 5th Semester Programs:  
Commerce, Business Administration:  

 
Business Administration: BBA 2 years or equivalent with at least second division in annual system 
or at least 2.5 CGPA out of 4 or 3.5 CGPA out of 5 from any institution recognized by HEC. 
Commerce: Must have passed B.Com. (2 years)/ ADC with at least 2nd division or CGPA 2.5/4.0.
