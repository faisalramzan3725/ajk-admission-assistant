Rules and Regulations for Hostel Residents:  
  
1. Hostel room once allotted is not allowed to change. However, only concerned authority may 
allow as special case on valid and reasonable grounds. Violation of this rule may be considered 
as act of misconduct and serious disciplinary actions will be initiated against violators.  
2. Students will show their identity card to hostel administration and security staff every time so 
required.  
3. The students are directed not to alter electric fitting/furniture etc. by himself.  
4. All switches must be put off while leaving the room i.e. lights, fans, extensions etc.  
5. Students should maintain cordial and harmonious relationships with fellow students.  
6. Before leaving the hostels, the students should complete the formalities and should leave hostel 
premises on his own risk/responsibility. No office staff will be held responsible for any/all kind 
of damages.  
7. All the male/ female students /residents are directed to strictly follow rules and regulations 
for residences of students / hostels  mentioned in official calendar of university, 
amended and enforced from time to time. 
Prohibition in the Hostel Premises:  
  
1. Fixing of posters making use of walls/doors for painting of various expressions etc. are strictly 
prohibited. Damaging of public p roperty is a very serious offence. The damage caused to the 
Hostel’s assets due to such activities will be recovered from residents of respective rooms.  
2. Using of air conditioners / air coolers, musical instruments and heaters are strictly prohibited.  
3. Tea/coffee making is not allowed in allotted rooms.  
4. Students are advised not to keep excess money/cash or any valuable in their allotted rooms.  
5. Students are strictly prohibited from forming any club/group/political/religious parties in the 
hostels.  
6. Students are prohibited from consuming alcoholic drinks, drugs or any other 
smoking/intoxicants inside the hostel premises. Any student found consuming above 
mentioned drugs will render himself liable for strict disciplinary action including 
expulsion/rustication from Hostel.  
7. Celebration of birthday and other parties are subject to prior approval from the concerned 
authority.
