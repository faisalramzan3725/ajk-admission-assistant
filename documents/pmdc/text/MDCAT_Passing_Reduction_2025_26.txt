NOTIFICATION
To address the issue ofvacantseats in MBBS and BDS programs fairly and systematically, the
matter, after deliberation at the national level, including the Health Standing Committees, has been
thoroughly reviewed by the PM&DC Council. Accordingly, it has been decided that:
1. Any admissions or processes already completed underthe Admission Regulations 2025 shall
remain valid and shall be treated as properly regulated.
2. Admitting universities that already have a pool of eligible MBBS and BDS students under
previously announced criteria must give priority to these students when filling vacant seats,
especially in private medical and dental institutions.
3. lf seats remain vacant after all students in the existing pool have been considered, and the
admitting universities are satisfied that no more eligible students are available, they may
allow a one-time reduction of up to 3% in the MDCAT passing percentage for both MBBS and
BDS admissions (i.e., MBBS: 52o/o and. BDS: 47o/o) for the financial stability of the institutions,
subject to the following conditions:
o Filling only the vacant seats for the academic session 2025-26
. Ensuring strict merit-based transparency and institutional accountability by the
admitting universities
o Monitoring the admission process by the admitting universities for the vacant seats
4. Furthermore, private institutions are encouraged to consider a reduction in tuition fees from
the currently notified / capped structure to enhance affordability and facilitate access to
medical and dental education for meritorious students.
