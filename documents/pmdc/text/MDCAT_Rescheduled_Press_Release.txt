PRESS RELEASE 
 
Islamabad: MDCAT Previously Scheduled for 16th August 2026 Rescheduled to 20th 
September 2026 
 
On the directions of the Hon’ble Prime Minister of Pakistan, the Pakistan Medical & Dental 
Council (PM&DC) hereby informs all candidates, parents, educational institutions and other 
stakeholders that the Medical and Dental College Admission Test (MDCAT), previously 
scheduled to be held on 16th August 2026, has been rescheduled to 20th September 2026. 
 
This decision has been taken to ensure the smooth, secure and transparent conduct of the 
examination while facilitating all candidates. 
 
All other terms, conditions and examination arrangements shall remain unchanged unless 
notified otherwise. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official PM&DC website and 
official communication channels for further updates and instructions. 
 
PM&DC appreciates the understanding and cooperation of all stakeholders and regrets any 
inconvenience caused.
