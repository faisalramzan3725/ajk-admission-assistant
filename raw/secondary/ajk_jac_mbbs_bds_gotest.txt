- Application completely online via JAC web portal; no paper applications.
- Eligibility: minimum 60% aggregate in FSc/A-level/equivalent HSSC; MDCAT minimum 55% (MBBS), 50% (BDS); First Class State Subject of AJ&K (exceptions for foreign / overseas categories).
- Merit weightage: SSC 10%, HSSC 40%, MDCAT 50%.
- Uploads: State Subject Class-I certificate, domicile, PRC, CNIC, photograph, signature, left thumb impression. 1947/1989 refugees have exemptions.
- Processing fee Rs. 3,000 + Rs. 400 IT charges (Easy Paisa / debit card / online banking).
- Timeline reported for 2026 session: applications 21-29 January; provisional merit list 5 February; first selection list 11 February; classes 1 March.
- Order of college preferences cannot be changed after submission.
