About Poonch Medical College
Poonch Medical College (PMC), Rawalakot Azad Jammu & Kashmir is a Public Sector Institution, a leading institution dedicated to excellence in medical education, research, and healthcare delivery. Since its establishment, PMC has been committed to training future healthcare professionals with strong academic foundations, ethical values, and a deep sense of responsibility toward humanity.
OUR VISION
To achieve excellence in medical education by nurturing skilled, compassionate healthcare professionals through innovative teaching. We strive to enhance our national ranking by upholding the highest standards in education, biomedical research, and patient care
OUR MISSION
To provide quality medical education that produces competent and compassionate health professionals who are committed to ethical values, research and lifelong learning
