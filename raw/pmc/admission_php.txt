Admission Procedure
Information regarding Entry Test and Admission Process through the official channels.
01
Entrance Test (MDCAT)
The entrance test is conducted by the
Admitting University
as designated by the Government.
Candidates must meet the minimum percentile/marks in MDCAT as per national policy.
F.Sc (Pre-Medical) with the required minimum percentage set by the regulatory authority.
02
Joint Admission Committee (JAC)
The selection process for AJ&K Medical Colleges is managed by
Joint Admission Committee (JAC) Muzaffarabad
:
Centralized admission process for all three public sector medical colleges of AJ&K.
Selection is based on the aggregate merit (Matric, F.Sc, and MDCAT weightage).
Final merit lists are notified by the JAC Muzaffarabad Secretariat.

Official MDCAT Result Card (Valid for current session).
State Subject & Domicile Certificate (AJ&K State).
Candidate's CNIC/B-Form and Guardian's CNIC.
