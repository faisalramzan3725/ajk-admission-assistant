UHS Degree & Transcript Issuance Portal
- Track application: Case Number + CNIC/Passport No.
- Initiate new application: UHS Registration No. + Father's Name + Case Type
- Case types: Degree Issuance; Duplicate Degree; Degree Verification; Bonafide Letter; Transcript Issuance; Transcript with Verification; Transcript Verification
- Principal / HOD Approval Form required (separate forms for Degree and Transcript), downloadable on the portal
- Portal lets you print fee voucher and upload the paid fee voucher
- Degree queries: Degree Cell 042-99231304-9 Ext. 395, 042-99231075, degreecell@uhs.edu.pk
- Transcript queries: Examination Cell 042-99231304-9 Ext. 356, 042-99231075, tab@uhs.edu.pk
