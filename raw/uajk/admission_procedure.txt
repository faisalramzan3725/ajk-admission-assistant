HOW TO APPLY – PROCEDURE
Candidates can apply online via the
UAJ&K Admissions Portal
or submit a hard copy of the application form directly at the Directorate of Students’ Affairs (DSA) office.
Hard copies of the application form are available at the DSA office.
Candidates must generate an online challan form of
PKR 3,000
(payable at any HBL Branch).
Candidates applying for more than one program must deposit an additional
PKR 1,000 per extra program
.
An entry test is required where applicable.
Entry test fee:
PKR 1,000
plus
PKR 200 per additional program
.
