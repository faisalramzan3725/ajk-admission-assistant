IMPORTANT NOTICE: NO SEPARATE CALL LETTERS OR INTIMATIONS FOR ENTRY TESTS – FALL 2026
All applicants seeking admission to the Fall 2026 Semester at the University of Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Muzaffarabad, are hereby informed that no separate call letters, SMS notifications, or individual intimations will be issued to candidates awaiting their entry tests.
Candidates are advised to appear for their respective entry tests as per the officially announced schedule, without waiting for any further communication from the University.

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR CANDIDATES
Proof of Fee Payment: All candidates who have already paid the entry test fee must present a copy of their paid fee challan to the examination staff for verification.
On-the-Spot Fee Payment: Candidates who have not yet paid the entry test fee may pay it at the examination venue by filling out the prescribed challan form and completing the payment formalities.
Timely Arrival: All candidates are advised to report to the examination venue well before their scheduled test time.
Please Note: No individual call letters or intimations will be issued. Candidates are responsible for following the announced schedule and appearing for their entry tests accordingly.
