Introduction
Quantifiable evaluation of one’s knowledge, skills, attitudes, and beliefs may be called as Educational Assessment. The word ‘assessment’ came into use in an educational context after the Second World War. (Nelson and Dawson, 2014).
Generally UAJ&K relies on Standardized Tests for the assessment of the candidates. However, in departments/ institutes following the semester system, Non-Standardized Tests are also being used by individual faculty members as innovative strategy. Depending upon the nature of the course/ program, our assessment methodology may vary.
