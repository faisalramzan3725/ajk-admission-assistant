Required Documents
Applicants must provide:
Attested copies of all educational certificates and degrees.
Character certificate issued by the College/ Institute last attended.
Migration certificate in case of Masters/ Graduation from institutions other than AJ&K University.
NOC/ Migration certificate in case of F.Sc. from boards other than
BISE Mirpur
AJ&K.
A copy of Domicile & State subject certificate.
A copy of National Identity Card or Form-B
Four recent passport size photographs.
Copy of Guardian’s CNIC
Important Notes
Applicants applying to Muzaffarabad Campus, King Abdullah Campus (Chatter Klass), Jhelum Valley Campus (Hattian Bala), or Neelum Campus (Athmuqam) must report to their campus for Entry Test (if required).
Candidates must bring original documents/ degrees for verification at the time of the interview.
Foreign students may apply directly to the University. However, their admission will only be confirmed upon submission of an NOC from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Pakistan.
Employees of public sector organizations must provide a study leave certificate or NOC from their department to pursue full-time studies.
