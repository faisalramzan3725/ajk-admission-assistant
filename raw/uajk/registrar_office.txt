Registrar’s Office
The Registrar is a full-time statutory officer and serves as the custodian of the University’s official seal and academic records.
The Office of the Registrar is responsible for maintaining the integrity and accuracy of academic documentation, including the graduate registry, and ensuring compliance with institutional regulations and procedures. It also oversees the conduct of elections for various University bodies in accordance with prescribed statutes.
In addition, the Registrar serves as Secretary to key statutory bodies, including the Syndicate, Academic Council, and Selection Board, facilitating effective governance, coordination, and decision-making processes within the University.
