Directorate Of Students Financial Assistance
The Directorate of Student Financial Assistance & University Advancement (SFA & UA) serves as a catalyst to support the University of Azad Jammu & Kashmir (UAJK) in fund raising, establishing public-private linkages, helping students meet financial requirements for their education, providing career services, and developing alumni engagement programs.
No student should discontinue his/ her university education due to financial constraints
Values of SFA & UA
The SFA & UA is committed to several key values that are reflected in all aspects of its operations:
Transparency and Integrity
Service to students
Effective management and accountability in its use of resources and implementation of programs
Active communication and outreach to economically disadvantaged students
Constant improvement of programs and services
Our Principles of Service
We aim to serve every student of UAJK and ensure high-quality service for all.
We strive to be informative, up-to-date, organized, and responsive.
We are friendly, courteous, and helpful, offering personalized attention.
We respect and protect the confidentiality of student information.
(a) Student Financial Assistance
Financial assistance is provided to enhance access to higher education, especially for talented but financially constrained students. The Student Financial Assistance section works to:
Streamline financial aid for meritorious and needy students.
Promote transparency in financial programs.
Build partnerships with industry, alumni, donors, and community organizations.

Prime Minister’s Tuition Fee Reimbursement Program
This scheme covers tuition and other mandatory fees for Master’s, M.Phil., and PhD students with valid AJK domicile enrolled at UAJK. Refugees 1989-90 settled in AJK are also included.
Coverage
Actual tuition fee
Admission, examination, registration, and lab charges
Last year: 1,580 students received Rs. 34.527 million in scholarships.
Pak-USAID Merit and Needs-Based Scholarship Program
Available at undergraduate and graduate levels for Sociology, Economics, Linguistics (English, Urdu, Kashmir Studies), and Psychology.
Eligibility
Must be a Pakistani/AJK national
50% scholarships reserved for females
Must be enrolled in first semester of respective degree
Coverage
Tuition Fee
Monthly Stipend
Last year: 95 students received Rs. 17.199 million in scholarships.
HEC Need-Based Scholarship Program
Provides financial support to deserving students of UAJK for undergraduate and graduate programs.
Eligibility
Enrolled UAJK students meeting need criteria
Coverage
Tuition Fee
Lodging, Transportation, Books, Academic Costs
Rs. 6,000 monthly stipend (max 10 months/year)
Last year: 260 students received Rs. 22.102 million.
Ihsan Trust Qarz-e-Hasna Scheme
This interest-free loan scheme supports needy and deserving students. Repayment is based on a student’s financial situation and begins during study with minimal amounts.
Maintain academic performance as per Trust policy
Monthly repayments are mandatory and monitored
Online deposits accepted via Meezan Bank
Last year: 08 students received Rs. 0.161 million.
Pakistan-French Need-Based Scholarship
Offered by the French Embassy to support deserving UAJK students at undergraduate and graduate levels.
Eligibility
Must be enrolled in the first professional year of a prescribed course
Coverage
Tuition Fee
Other academic expenses
Last year: 15 students received Rs. 0.750 million.
HBL Foundation Scholarship
Launched in collaboration with HBL Foundation for undergraduate students.
Minimum 65% marks in F.Sc/F.A
Admission on open merit
Not receiving any other scholarship

(c) Career Services
The Career Services section facilitates career planning, internships, and job placement. It organizes annual job fairs and collaborates with industries to create job opportunities for students.
